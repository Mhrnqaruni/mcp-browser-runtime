# Benchmarks

No validated comparative speed, token-use, task-success, or latency benchmark is
published yet. Capture profiles and response ceilings are implemented features,
not evidence of superiority over another MCP implementation.

Before reporting numbers, use a pinned synthetic corpus, versions, tokenizer and
hardware; record repeated runs, raw JSON/CSV, median/p95, failures, truncation and
information loss. Do not derive performance claims from one successful demo.
